# Monetary Unit, Banking Layer, and Mandate

Three questions raised by the drafted whitepaper Sections 5–9 are decided by the user. They resolve
`OPEN-21`, `OPEN-22`, and `OPEN-23` in
[whitepaper/R-white-paper-v0.2.md](../../whitepaper/R-white-paper-v0.2.md) and follow
[[DEC-004-monetary-authority-and-authored-decline]].

## Confirmed — R is the world's monetary unit, assumed to exist from the outset

R is **the monetary unit of THE RESERVE's world**. A certain quantity is **assumed to have existed from the
beginning**.

The base is therefore a premise of the world rather than an act of the institution. R is not a liability
of THE RESERVE, is issued against nothing, and represents no claim on the institution. THE RESERVE has no
balance sheet in which R appears as an obligation.

This settles `OPEN-21` and reframes genesis. The institution does not create the monetary base; it holds
a quantity that the world assumes, records it, and releases from it. "Issuing authority" in
[[DEC-004-monetary-authority-and-authored-decline]] means that no one else may bring R into existence — not
that the institution performed an act of creation.

### Refinement — a dated initial issuance

The user subsequently clarifies that R was **first issued at a specific point in the past, in a fixed
quantity**, with no further issuance since and continuous decrease thereafter.

This locates the assumption rather than replacing it. The base is still not created by any act the
document describes, and R is still not a liability. What changes is that genesis is a **past event in the
world's history**, not the beginning of the record a reader encounters: the decline is already under way
when the work is met. No date, quantity, or elapsed interval is supplied.

## Confirmed — the same institution operates the banking layer

THE RESERVE operates the banking layer itself. There are no separately chartered or licensed banks, and no
second institution.

The user states the governing reason directly: **the world is not to be expanded unnecessarily.** This is a
worldbuilding principle, not only an answer to this question, and it applies to later decisions about
whether to add institutions, roles, or mechanisms.

The consequence is that the separation of base money from claims denominated in it — the design principle
recorded in [[r-monetary-protocol]] — is a separation of **layers**, not of institutions. The same
institution holds the base and would operate any deposit or credit function above it.

## Confirmed — the mandate is RESERVE

The mandate of the institution is **RESERVE**: to reserve, to keep, to preserve what is held.

It is not price stability, employment, growth, liquidity, or activity. Because release is the
institution's only discretionary monetary act (see the consequences in
[[DEC-004-monetary-authority-and-authored-decline]]), the mandate is the criterion against which release is
judged, and it is the whole of the institution's stated monetary objective.

This also supplies the motive the earlier drafting could not state: the institution's purpose is
keeping, which is the same concern the confirmed bio records in [[DEC-002-public-language]] and the
retention direction records in [[DEC-003-currency-and-circulation-direction]].

## Consequences and one open reading

- A holder holds the unit itself, not a claim on anyone. What the institution owes is the preservation of
  the unit's conditions, not a payment. What a holder can do that a non-holder cannot is still unstated.
- One institution now holds the base and would also create claims denominated in it. Whether the banking
  layer may create claims exceeding the base it holds is unspecified and consequential.
- A mandate to reserve sits against a mechanism whose central act is destroying money. This was recorded
  here as `OPEN-27` alongside an assistant reading — that retirement removes only the undistributed
  remainder, so what is preserved is what people hold. **The user corrected that reading**; see
  [[DEC-006-burn-mechanism-and-value-preservation]]. R's value is falling and destruction is how it is
  preserved, so the mandate and the mechanism share one purpose. The superseded reading is retained here
  as the question's history, not as a current claim.

## Boundaries

These are world and design decisions. They confirm no parameter, approve no release, and establish no
legal entity, regulatory classification, banking licence, or actual monetary function outside the work.
The limits in [[DEC-003-currency-and-circulation-direction]] and
[[DEC-004-monetary-authority-and-authored-decline]] remain in force.
