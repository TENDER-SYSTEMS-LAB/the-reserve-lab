# THE RESERVE

**A place for what remains.**

The project name and public bio are confirmed by the user. See [[DEC-001-project-name]] and [[DEC-002-public-language]].

## Working field

The supplied conversations explore a bank artwork through custody, trust, credit, obligations, and guarantees. ChatGPT's strongest recommendation is an institution that assesses future claims and turns eligible promises into present credit. This is a candidate direction, not an adopted operating model.

The naming conversation also explores keeping, accumulation, and what remains. The approved bio does not settle what is deposited, whether custody is the central mechanism, or whether future credit is the selected direction. Preserve that distinction when developing the work.

## Design readiness

The 2026-09-06 audit places the work at public-identity selection and institutional comparison, before a selected operating contract. Its proposed next test is to explain what the institution makes possible for a visitor, what obligations remain, and what the institution itself owes. Neither the depth of the original banking proposal nor the approved bio answers those questions.

The audit recommends an institution-centered scope with deep rules only where they change the core experience. This is a review proposal, not an adopted scope. Shared world context in the user's audit brief does not imply a shared currency, common accounts, simultaneous release, or a central-bank role.

## Evidence boundary

The bank interpretation rests on one assistant response to a missing attachment. The naming conversation supplies direct user confirmation of the name and bio, not of the financial model. Neither a functioning service nor a validated calculation model is established by these sources.

The later audit adds a dated synthesis and testable planning proposals, not a new user selection of a mechanism. Its sibling-repository findings are secondary evidence here; the full report records the inspected commits, uncommitted snapshots, and access limits.
