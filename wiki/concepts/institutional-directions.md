# Institutional Directions

All five directions below were proposed by ChatGPT. No selection by the user appears in this source. They are alternatives for one bank artwork, not five approved projects or a roadmap.

| Direction | Institutional action and central tension | Proposed mechanisms | Main risk identified in the source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Future Claims Bank | Assess which promises can support present credit; bind the future self through a present contract | Underwriting, discounting, collateral, maturity, default, refinancing | Turning time or the future into a literal commodity or science-fiction device |
| Credibility Institute | Evaluate a submitted claim; ask how much evidence trust can demand before it ceases to be trust | Verification, claim ratings, guarantees, performance history | Scoring whole people or reproducing a social-credit dystopia |
| Custody Institution | Protect something by taking control of access; ask what preservation requires surrendering | Lock-up, withdrawal limits, redemption, pooling, liquidity and run risk | A generic memory bank, or duplicating an ownership-centered artwork |
| Clearing House of Obligations | Record and net obligations; ask which debts can truly be settled | Bilateral/multilateral netting, reconciliation, settlement finality, counterparty risk | Abstraction that makes the interaction difficult to grasp |
| Guarantee / Last Resort Institution | Stand behind promises and contain their failure; ask who guarantees the guarantor | Coverage, reserves, collateral, default funds, contagion and stress testing | Starting at an infrastructure scale too remote from the visitor |

## Recommendation in the source

ChatGPT ranks Future Claims Bank strongest, linking a promise, eligibility assessment, present credit, time, and contractual consequences. [[future-claims]] preserves that proposal in more detail. The source also describes clearing as potentially the most distinctive independent artwork; this does not override its explicit overall recommendation.
