# Future Claims

This is ChatGPT's recommended candidate among [[institutional-directions]]. It is not a user-selected design or an implemented system.

## Proposed purpose

Assess, finance, and administer eligible future claims. The visitor acts as an applicant or borrower, submitting a commitment to a future action or condition. The institution decides how much present credit that specific claim can support. The intended question concerns the credibility of a future commitment rather than its market price.

## Proposed interaction

1. Submit a claim with a maturity date and supporting evidence.
2. Evaluate consistency, dependencies, verification quality, external volatility, collateral availability, and non-performance risk.
3. Return terms such as advance rate, credit limit, collateral requirement, maturity, and risk grade.
4. Let time and new information change the account: interest accrues, risk is reassessed, conditions can be breached, and claims can mature or default.

The source sketches Future Credit, Secured Future Credit, and Term Commitment as possible initial products, with refinancing and restructuring later. None is approved. Sample percentages, dates, ratings, and balances in the response are illustrative UI values, not computed or calibrated results. No formula or functioning assessment model is supplied.

## Contract and human consequence

The response proposes evaluating claims rather than assigning a total score to a person. It distinguishes contractual performance from human fulfillment: a contract might be marked fully performed while the person experiences failure. Default could reflect broken promises, changed circumstances, or a commitment that has lost meaning. Actual stakes, recovery, renegotiation, and discharge remain open in [[Q-001-institutional-scope]].

## Proposed artistic constraints

- Build state changes, calculations, and consequences into interaction; a banking skin over poetic text is insufficient in this proposal.
- Use contractual states rather than reward points. Proposed examples include Performing, Delinquent, Under Review, Matured, and Defaulted.
- Make the institution competent and internally consistent; avoid relying on a villainous corporation or a single anti-capitalist moral.
- Keep the surface understandable without requiring visitors to learn the calculation model.
- Avoid literal purchases of years of life, emotion scores, and explanatory dream-deposit slogans.

These constraints are ChatGPT recommendations, not confirmed project rules.

## Proposed worldbuilding boundary

The response recommends revealing partial monetary mechanics: balances, lending, interest, settlement, collateral, and reference rates, while leaving issuance, backing, redemption, legal context, and the shared unit's origin unresolved. It suggests subtle infrastructure traces across works, such as a financing notice, settlement institution, reference rate, or recurring unit, rather than an explicit crossover story.

A shared currency, the labels TENDER/TDR, actual convertibility, and further insurance/audit/exchange institutions are not adopted. The response itself advises against turning its expansion examples into a roadmap and recommends building only lore with operational consequences.

## Research still required

The source calls for careful study of credit, interest, discounting, collateral, maturity, default, liquidity, and money creation before specifying the system. It warns against treating interest as only waiting or explaining banking solely as relending a fraction of deposits. These are unverified research prompts from the conversation; ingestion has not validated financial claims or models.
