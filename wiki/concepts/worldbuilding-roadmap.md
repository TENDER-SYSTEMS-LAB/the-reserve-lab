## Proposed scope

Scope determines which relationships are included. Depth determines how fully they work. Exposure determines what visitors need to see. Implementation determines what must execute. An internal rule can be precise without being fully displayed; an undecided rule cannot be treated as deliberate mystery.

| Scope option | Artistic benefit | Additional decisions and dependencies | Maintenance and overlap | Assessment in the report |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Institution-centered** | Makes one visitor–institution relationship tangible; tests independent participation | One core action, eligible object, contract, result, ending, and necessary external premises | Concentrates on one contract; time and storage may still need exact rules. Application/status screens alone do not distinguish it from other works | Recommended working scope because the unresolved need is the institution's action, not the economy's origin |
| Include shared infrastructure | Consistent references; if actual transfer is chosen, a right can be used or redeemed elsewhere | References need agreed meaning, canonical source, and version. Transfer adds accepting parties, ledger responsibility, cancellation, settlement; account integration is another choice | Reference upkeep is limited; transfers add failures across systems; accounts add recovery. Shared references remain compatible with institution-centered scope | Appropriate if an actual connection is essential; its necessity and approval are currently unresolved |
| Include economic and social systems | Can make issuance, guarantees, distribution, or institutional legitimacy the central subject | Multiple parties, authority, obligations, resources, and only the history or jurisdiction that affects results | New promises change other rules; wider validation and overlap with other works. Background may grow while the core action remains undefined | Reconsider if issuance or final guarantees become the artistic question |

The proposed first comparison tests whether a visitor's own future performance can justify a useful present right; [[future-claims]] retains the candidate's unfilled benefit and performance criteria. Custody is the nearest alternative if delegation of control and return matter more. Assessment needs an actual effect beyond a rating; clearing needs understandable multiple-party obligations; guarantees need the institution to bear a meaningful failure. These are tests for form, not legal classifications or adopted roles.

## Proposed target depth, exposure, and implementation

- **D0:** Do not design in this scope; distinguish unnecessary material from deliberate deferral.
- **D1:** Define existence, role, and essential premises.
- **D2:** Define consistent normal flows, inputs/results, rights/obligations, and core constraints.
- **D3:** Define state transitions, time, necessary calculations, exceptions, and verification cases.

## Proposed completion criteria

All six conditions below establish readiness to build the core experience, not completion of the artwork. Lightweight sketches can precede this point.

1. **Institutional necessity:** One case explains who applies, what becomes possible, who owes what to whom, and the institution's limits even without banking titles or layouts.
2. **Traceable action:** Input, decision grounds, acceptance, created rights/obligations, elapsed time, and residual records form one connected path. Numbers and states have a rule-based origin.
3. **Consistent representative cases:** Applicable normal, refusal, maturity, cancellation/exit, and non-performance/correction cases end under the same rules. New products or institutions do not conceal exceptions.
4. **Defined internal rules:** Makers know hidden judgment criteria. Unchosen clocks, benefits, or redemption rules are recorded as unresolved, not disguised as mystery.
5. **Necessary dependencies only:** A visitor can participate independently. Shared references have a meaning, canonical source, and version; assess the core path when a referenced work is unavailable.
6. **User judgment and observed evidence:** The user has reviewed core role, object, consequences, and exposure choices. Observations support whether visitors understand their choices and experience the tension between institutional performance and personal meaning. Expected reactions are not test results.

Before adding a rule, identify the representative input, decision, right, or result it changes. History or the surrounding world can itself become the artistic question, but that requires a scope comparison rather than an automatic addition.

## Proposed sequence and gates

The detailed hypothetical cases and their unresolved P/B/O/M definitions live in [[future-claims]]. The report performed gap analysis, not audience testing or code execution. Each future output begins as a review artifact; concrete user choices remain in [[Q-001-institutional-scope]] until supported by decision evidence.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. | **B. | **D. | **E. | **F. A → B → C → D → E → F is conditional. C can return to A/B, E to D, and any essential external use/redemption requirement reopens scope and shared-contract decisions. Ingestion does not itself authorize implementing or releasing these stages.

Evidence/approval checks, cross-work boundaries, confirmed wording, object/record/right distinctions, and exposure criteria can proceed regardless of institutional type. Look for the missing old prompt if available, but do not make its recovery a condition for completing review work.

Type-dependent rules follow the relationship: credit needs benefit/performance/maturity; custody needs delegated power/return; clearing needs parties/closure; guarantees need resources/failure; assessment needs a meaningful effect. Accounts, ledgers, and formulas follow these choices.

A and the dependency review can run in parallel. Once B supplies minimum rules, lightweight flows and public conditions can be compared together. Fixing storage/clocks before C, or implementing E alongside B, would embed untested assumptions. A and C carry the greatest artistic uncertainty; B and D carry consistency work; E's cost depends on medium, time, and persistence. No dates or development durations are inferred.
